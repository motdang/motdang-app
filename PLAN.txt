MOT DANG — THE APP THAT DOES WHAT BEER DOES
Plan, 10 Oct 2026. For Nan and Marcus. Nothing here is built yet.


THE APP IN ONE LINE

You ask for something in English or Thai. The app finds the right place. If that
is not enough, a helper calls around in Thai and tells you what they found.


WHO USES IT

  Customers   Americans and other farang without a Thai partner. Thai people.
  Providers   Thai shops, clinics, labs, tradespeople.
  Helpers     Beer, Marcus, Marcus's friends (Thai, English; some Shan).


THE LOOP  (from the lab search Beer did for Nan)

  1  Ask        "a lab that can analyze compounds"
  2  List       motdang + Google Maps + Google + what a local knows
  3  Calls      a helper calls down the list in Thai
  4  Summary    who does it, who doesn't, price, when, the closest match
  5  Book       or stop: "none of them does this" is an answer too
  6  Back in    each call goes into motdang as "checked by phone, <date>",
                so the next person gets the answer without a call

SCREENS — CUSTOMER  (four, one big button each)

  1  ASK      One box, one camera button. Language follows the phone.
              Under the box, three examples that change:
              "plumber tomorrow" · "dentist open Sunday" · "bus to Chiang Rai"

  2  ANSWER   Up to five places. Each one shows: name in English and Thai,
              open now or not, price if known, how far, last checked.
              Buttons on each: Call · Directions · What to say.
              For an errand (license, bank account): the steps and what to bring.
              At the bottom, the big button:  [ Have someone call around ]

  3  JOB      Like waiting for a Grab: the helper's photo and name,
              "calling 3 of 7", and a chat with the helper.

  4  RESULT   The summary. Buttons: Book · Send someone · Done.


SCREENS — HELPER  (three)

  1  JOBS     New jobs, mine, finished.
  2  CALLING  The list of places, tap to call, the Thai script on screen.
              After each call, four taps: does it? · price · when · note.
  3  SUMMARY  Written from those taps. The helper fixes it and sends.


SCREENS — NAN  (one)

  Jobs, money in, helpers paid, and what went back into motdang.


SCREENS — PROVIDERS

  Later (phase 4): claim the listing, set hours and prices, get bookings.


NO MANUAL — HOW WE CHECK

  - One screen, one job, one big button.
  - Buttons are words people say: Ask, Call, Book, Done.
  - Five people who have never seen it, one task each
    ("find a plumber for tomorrow"). Every question they ask
    changes a screen. Thai testers and farang testers, separately.
  - Thai words written by Beer or Marcus, not machine translation.

BUILD ORDER

  Phase 0  By hand, no code. The ask box on motdang sends the ask to the
           helpers on LINE. Beer or Marcus searches and calls by hand and
           replies with a summary. Ten jobs; Nan's own first. Time each one:
           that number sets the price.

  Phase 1  Ask + Answer screens on motdang's data. The big button starts
           the Phase 0 flow.

  Phase 2  Helper screens, the four-tap call form, the summary, job status.
           Call results written back into motdang.

  Phase 3  Someone to the house. A roster of the plumbers, maids and
           gardeners the helpers have used and would send again. Booking.
           A helper goes along the first time. Shan helps here.

  Phase 4  Providers claim their listing, set hours and prices, take bookings.

  Phase 5  Errands: steps for the license, bank account, PO box, bus to
           Chiang Rai; 7-Eleven; ordering on Grab and LINE MAN; the camera
           reading Thai.


DECISIONS FOR NAN

  1  Price per job.
       a  Free for Phase 0, priced after ten timed jobs.
       b  Flat price per call-around.
       c  By the hour.
     Too low and helpers burn out; too high and nobody tries it.

  2  Helper pay: per job or per hour; paid by motdang.net Co., Ltd.?

  3  Where it lives.
       a  A web app on motdang.net, added to the home screen, wrapped in
          this Android app. Fastest; works for both.
       b  A LINE Official Account. Thai people live in LINE; LINE charges
          for messages past a free monthly number.
       c  Both: web first, LINE as a second door.

  4  Name and address. A new public name or domain is yours.

  5  Google search inside the app.
       a  Google's Places API: paid per search.
       b  The helper searches Google by hand, as Beer does now.

  6  Money.
       a  How customers pay: PromptPay to the company for Thai customers;
          card or PayPal for farang.
       b  Whether a helper ever pays a provider for a customer. That means
          holding the customer's money.

  7  Who does the first ten jobs: Beer, Marcus, or both.

  8  Who sees a job. Jobs hold home addresses and health questions
     (pap smear, dentist). Helpers only, helpers + Nan, or wider.
